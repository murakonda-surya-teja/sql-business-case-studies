## 📊 Business Analytics Scenarios

The portfolio will cover the following analytical scenarios:

### 1. Sales & Revenue Analytics
- Calculate total business revenue.
- Identify top-performing products.
- Analyze monthly revenue trends.
- Compare regional sales performance.

### 2. Customer Analytics
- Identify high-value customers.
- Analyze customer purchase frequency.
- Calculate average customer spending.
- Identify inactive customers.

### 3. Employee Analytics
- Identify highest-paid employees.
- Calculate department-wise salaries.
- Rank employees by performance metrics.

### 4. Financial Analytics
- Analyze monthly expenses.
- Identify top expense categories.
- Calculate revenue and profit margins.

### 5. Data Quality Analysis
- Identify duplicate records.
- Find missing values.
- Validate business data.
- Detect inconsistent records.
